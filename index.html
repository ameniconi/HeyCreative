Why we started

We wanted to build something on our own terms.

Like a lot of people, we've spent years building careers around work. But eventually we realised we wanted to create something that worked around our family and the life we actually wanted to live.

So we decided to combine what we're good at and build something of our own.

Hey Creative gives us the freedom to work flexibly while doing work we genuinely enjoy — and hopefully helping other independent businesses gain a little more freedom of their own.

We understand what it's like to build something from scratch, work with limited budgets and have to make every decision count.
